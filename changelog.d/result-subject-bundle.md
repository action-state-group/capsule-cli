- **Added:** the vendored Evidence Result v0 schema carries aac #674's optional claim member `subject_bundle` (spec section 4.5, aac `4ed9a4c6`), so `capsulectl result build` and the book plugin's `book result build` seal a Result whose claim names, by its bundle digest, the Evidence Bundle that shows the claim's subject. It is display-only: nothing in capsulectl reads it, it is not a citation (the named Bundle need not be in the log and is not among the sealed Capsule's references), and a malformed one (a bare digest, uppercase hex, another `digest_alg`, an extra member) is still refused (capsule-cli#473).
