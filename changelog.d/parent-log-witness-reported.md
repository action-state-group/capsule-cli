- `capsulectl verify --bundle` reports a `parent-log-witness/v1` extension (Evidence Bundle -03), the neutral form of a log's witnessed checkpoint: its `extensions` entry carries a `parent_witness` object (status, findings, receipts and, when it passed, the covered extent, whole when `entries` equals `of`), the status follows agent-action-capsule's reading against the run's `--witness-directory`, and a failing chain makes the verdict INVALID; a withheld one (no directory, no receipts) is shown and does not gate (capsule-cli#608). The chain's extent is computed from the verified checkpoints, never read from the block.
