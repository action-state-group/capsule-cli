- **Fixed:** a witness delivery that reached the witness but got no answer before its deadline is no longer worded as missing network consent. It is recorded as timed out, its pending reason is `witness_timeout` ("pending: the witness was reached but its answer did not come in time, so the witness or the network to it is slow; …"), and it is retried as before. A request that never got a connection to the witness (refused, unresolved, a proxy that holds or refuses the tunnel, a TLS handshake that never finishes) keeps `network_consent_needed`. `capsulectl doctor --check-witness` makes the same split: a probe that connected but timed out reports `timed_out: true` and `reachable: false`, since no answer came back, and says the witness or the network to it is slow, with the reached text rather than the consent-needed one (#643).
