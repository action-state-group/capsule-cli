- **Fixed:** a witness delivery that reached the witness but got no answer before its deadline is no longer worded as missing network consent. It is recorded as timed out, its pending reason is `witness_timeout` ("pending: the witness was reached but did not answer in time, so it is slow or busy; …"), and it is retried as before. A request that never got a connection to the witness (refused, unresolved, a proxy that holds or refuses the tunnel, a TLS handshake that never finishes) keeps `network_consent_needed`. `capsulectl doctor --check-witness` makes the same split: a probe that connected but timed out reports `timed_out: true` and says the witness is slow or busy, with no consent text (#643).
