- **Breaking:** `verify --bundle` names the signature forms of a composed bundle's refusal and artifact-response members by neutral ids (agent-action-capsule#676): `three-member-refusal/v1` (was `evidencebook-refusal`), `digest-bound-artifact-response/v1` (was `evidencebook-artifact-response`) and `whole-refusal/v1` (was `ed25519-jcs`) in the `refusal_signature` and `response_signature` `profile` members. Go API: `ThreeMemberRefusalProfile`, `DigestBoundResponseProfile` and `WholeRefusalProfile` replace `EvidenceBookRefusalProfile`, `ResponseSignatureProfile` and `RefusalSignatureProfile`; a plugin that names them updates its pin (capsule-cli#428).
