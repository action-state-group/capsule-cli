- **Fixed:** `verify --history` on a stapled commitment whose checks all pass except the two it cannot make (`request_binding` is `NOT_PRESENT`, `caller_invariance` is `NOT_CHECKED`) no longer says "This history wasn't accepted. The findings list the checks that didn't pass." with an empty list. It says "This card checks out, but it answers no request of yours, so it isn't accepted as an answer." and exits 3 ("nothing failed, but something is left"), not 1. The verdict is still `NOT_ACCEPTED`; a staple with a failed check keeps its wording and exit 1. A wording pack pinned with `profile update --wording` needs the new key `say.verify.history_answers_no_request`, or it falls back to the built-in pack (capsule-cli#548).
