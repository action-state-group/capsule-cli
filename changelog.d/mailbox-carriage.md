- **Added:** `mailbox ask`, `mailbox poll`, `mailbox inbox` and `mailbox reply` carry an evidence request and its signed answer over the Nostr mailbox a commitment names (`ask.nostr`), as agent-action-capsule's `nostr-mailbox-v0` binding describes: the asker takes the mailbox only from a commitment under the key it holds for the responder; `poll` accepts a reply only by the binding's checks 1 to 7 or by content under the responder's own key for this request, refuses an answer not bound to the request's digest, and keeps a reply that arrives after the deadline beside the absence. The request, the answer and their recording stay with `request`, `respond` and `request --response`. The binding's vectors are checked byte for byte (capsule-cli#573).
